# Introduction

![ItemBlocker](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/kuqk/ItemBlocker/main/.github/banner.png)

ItemBlocker controls what players may do with items on Paper, Folia, Purpur and Spigot servers, Minecraft 1.20 to 26.3.

* **Block items per action**: crafting, picking up, dropping, using, placing, wearing, moving in containers, hoppers, smithing, attacking, breaking, crafters, dispensers, brewing, trading, loot and mob drops.
* **Let players keep an item but not get more of it** with the `obtain` keyword.
* **Block one specific item**, such as a sword from an RPG plugin, by its name, lore, custom model data, plugin data or enchantments.
* **Block whole groups** of items, such as every sword, and allow just a few of them.
* **Limit** how big an item stacks and how many of it a player may carry.
* **Enchantments**: block items above a level, or lower the enchantment, remove it or confiscate the item instead.
* **Potion effects** from a level.
* **Presets** that apply in some worlds or WorldGuard regions only, with ready-made templates for Survival, Lifesteal and PvP.
* **Exceptions**: let a rank ignore one rule or a whole preset.
* **A menu** for nearly everything, commands with tab completion, or one YAML file.
* **Scan** inventories and containers for items that are already there, and remove or confiscate them.
* **Logs** of every blocked action, with optional Discord alerts.
* **An API** for other plugins, with temporary rules and an event that can let actions through.
* Messages in English, Polish, German, Spanish, French and Italian.
